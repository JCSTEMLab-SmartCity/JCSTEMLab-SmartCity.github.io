Embodied AI & Low Altitude Economy

We connect embodied intelligence with autonomous driving and low-altitude systems through collaborative perception, navigation, and integrated air-ground design.

Embodied AI & Low Altitude Economy

Collaborative Perception

  • Designing collaborative perception and multi-agent scene understanding methods for embodied agents and autonomous vehicles in complex environments.
  • Overcoming occlusion, limited sensing range, and communication constraints through cooperative visual and multimodal perception.
  • Studying robust and secure collaboration mechanisms for perception, reasoning, and decision making in real-world deployments.

UAV Delivery

  • Developing intelligent UAV systems for delivery, inspection, and autonomous mission execution in smart-city environments.
  • Studying UGV-UAV collaboration, task coordination, and low-altitude autonomy for logistics and urban services.
  • Integrating perception, navigation, and scheduling for reliable autonomous systems under dynamic operating conditions.

Integrated Air-Ground Design

  • Exploring integrated air-ground architectures for sensing, communication, computing, and control in low-altitude economy applications.
  • Leveraging aerial and ground resources jointly to support smart mobility, traffic management, and responsive urban services.
  • Designing system-level frameworks that connect embodied intelligence with smart-city infrastructure and future air-ground ecosystems.
