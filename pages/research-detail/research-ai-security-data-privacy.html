AI Security

We build trustworthy AI systems by studying robustness, intellectual property protection, and forensic analysis for modern generative models.

AI Security

Adversarial Attack and Defense

  • Studying adversarial attacks and defenses for collaborative perception, embodied intelligence, and multimodal AI systems.
  • Analyzing robustness under malicious perturbations, distribution shifts, and adversarial interaction settings.
  • Designing defense mechanisms that preserve reliability, safety, and trustworthy deployment in real-world AI pipelines.

Model Watermarking

  • Developing secure model watermarking techniques for AI intellectual property protection, especially for generative models.
  • Studying robustness, fidelity, and resistance to removal attacks in practical white-box and black-box settings.
  • Exploring trustworthy ownership verification for modern foundation models and AIGC systems.

AIGC Forensics

  • Exploring AIGC forensics for synthetic image, video, and model output analysis in security-sensitive settings.
  • Studying data privacy, provenance, and tamper detection for trustworthy AI-generated content.
  • Connecting forensic analysis with privacy-preserving data transactions and secure AI deployment.
