Foundation Models

Two complementary thrusts: LLM agentic reasoning and systems for complex tasks; World Foundation Models for Spatial Intelligence as the cognitive core for Embodied AI (e.g., autonomous vehicles).

Large Language Models (LLMs)

Agentic Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning

  • Designing agentic reasoning and reinforcement learning methods to improve the performance of LLMs.
  • Exploring how LLMs can iteratively plan, reflect, and act in complex environments through chain-of-thought and self-correction mechanisms.
  • Integrating reinforcement learning (RLHF, RLAIF) to align LLM outputs with human preferences and task objectives.

Agentic Systems

  • Designing agentic systems (single/multi-agent) that can achieve complex tasks.
  • Building single-agent systems that autonomously decompose goals, use tools, and execute multi-step plans.
  • Developing multi-agent frameworks for collaboration, negotiation, and emergent problem-solving in open-ended environments.

PEFT and Task Adaptation

  • Designing PEFT methods and task-adaptation strategies to adapt LLMs to specific tasks better and more efficiently.
  • Low-rank adaptation (LoRA), adapter modules, and prompt tuning for parameter-efficient fine-tuning.
  • Task-specific adaptation for embodied AI, robotics, and domain-specific applications with limited compute.

World Foundation Models (WFMs)

World Understanding

  • Building World Foundation Models that understand the physical world from visual observations, sparse sensor streams, and multi-agent interactions.
  • Focusing on collaborative perception, 3D reconstruction, and scene understanding to recover geometry, semantics, and dynamics for spatial intelligence.
  • Leveraging 3D foundation models such as VGGT to support scalable 3D understanding, world representation, and downstream reasoning for embodied AI and autonomous driving.

World Generation

  • Designing generative World Foundation Models that synthesize plausible future worlds and enrich the predictive capacity of intelligent agents.
  • Studying controllable image and video generation from structured conditions, as well as generative image restoration, by leveraging generative foundation models such as Stable Diffusion and FLUX for world simulation and future prediction.
  • Supporting scene completion, what-if reasoning, and foresightful planning by generating realistic observations beyond what is directly seen.
