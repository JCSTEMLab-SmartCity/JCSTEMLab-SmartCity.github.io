Foundation Models

Two complementary thrusts: LLM agentic reasoning and systems for complex tasks; World Models for Spatial Intelligence as the cognitive core for Embodied AI (e.g., autonomous vehicles).

Large Language Models (LLMs)

Agentic Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning

  • Designing agentic reasoning and reinforcement learning methods to improve the performance of LLMs.
  • Exploring how LLMs can iteratively plan, reflect, and act in complex environments through chain-of-thought and self-correction mechanisms.
  • Integrating reinforcement learning (RLHF, RLAIF) to align LLM outputs with human preferences and task objectives.

Agentic Systems

  • Designing agentic systems (single/multi-agent) that can achieve complex tasks.
  • Building single-agent systems that autonomously decompose goals, use tools, and execute multi-step plans.
  • Developing multi-agent frameworks for collaboration, negotiation, and emergent problem-solving in open-ended environments.

PEFT and Task Adaptation

  • Designing PEFT methods and task-adaptation strategies to adapt LLMs to specific tasks better and more efficiently.
  • Low-rank adaptation (LoRA), adapter modules, and prompt tuning for parameter-efficient fine-tuning.
  • Task-specific adaptation for embodied AI, robotics, and domain-specific applications with limited compute.

World Models and Embodied AI

Spatial Intelligence for Embodied AI

  • Constructing World Models for Spatial Intelligence as the cognitive core for Embodied AI and autonomous systems.
  • Learning unified representations from visual observations, sparse sensor streams, and multi-agent interactions to perceive 3D scenes and reason about dynamics.
  • Enabling agents to anticipate how the world evolves, predict outcomes, and support safe planning and decision-making in complex environments.

Collaborative 3D Vision for Scalable World Modeling

  • Leveraging collaborative perception to expand the spatial coverage and reliability of a World Model beyond a single ego view.
  • Recovering occluded structures, fusing complementary observations, and maintaining robust 3D understanding across agents.
  • Addressing adversarial, bandwidth-constrained, and real-world driving conditions through efficient multi-agent information sharing and fusion.

Generative 3D Vision for Predictive World Modeling

  • Designing generative World Models that enrich the temporal and predictive capacity of embodied agents.
  • Combining generative foundation models such as Stable Diffusion, 3D foundation models such as VGGT, and 3D Gaussian Splatting for image generation, geometry understanding, and dynamic scene reconstruction.
  • Predicting plausible future states of the environment to support what-if analysis and foresightful planning for downstream tasks.
