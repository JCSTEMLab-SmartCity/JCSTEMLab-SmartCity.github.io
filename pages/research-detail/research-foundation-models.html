Agentic AI

We study agentic intelligence for large language and multimodal models, with an emphasis on reasoning, planning, reflection, and efficient task adaptation.

Agentic AI

Agentic RL

  • Designing agentic reinforcement learning and reasoning strategies that improve planning, reflection, and multi-step decision making for intelligent agents.
  • Studying how LLMs can iteratively think, critique, and act in complex environments through self-correction and long-horizon interaction.
  • Integrating reinforcement learning and feedback-driven alignment to improve robustness, usefulness, and task-level performance.

LLM/MLLM Reasoning

  • Advancing LLM and MLLM reasoning for tool use, perception-aware planning, and complex problem solving.
  • Developing models that connect language, vision, and structured context for grounded reasoning in open-ended environments.
  • Exploring chain-of-thought, test-time scaling, and reasoning augmentation for embodied and real-world tasks.

Efficient LLM

  • Designing efficient adaptation strategies for large models, including PEFT, prompt tuning, and low-rank fine-tuning.
  • Improving deployment efficiency through lightweight adaptation, task specialization, and compute-aware model design.
  • Supporting practical LLM usage in resource-constrained settings such as edge intelligence, robotics, and domain-specific applications.
