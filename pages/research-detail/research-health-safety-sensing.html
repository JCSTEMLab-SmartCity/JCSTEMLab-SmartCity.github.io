AI for Healthcare

We develop AI and sensing systems for healthcare, elderly care, and public safety using multimodal signals and privacy-preserving wireless sensing.

AI for Healthcare

Multimodal Health Monitoring

  • Developing multimodal health monitoring systems that integrate physiological, visual, audio, and environmental signals for smart healthcare.
  • Studying remote and outdoor health sensing for elderly care, chronic disease monitoring, and public-health support.
  • Exploring non-invasive monitoring technologies such as camera-based sensing and wireless physiological measurement.

Wireless Sensing for Public Safety

  • Using wireless sensing and AI to monitor public environments for safety, anomaly detection, and rapid risk awareness.
  • Designing privacy-preserving sensing systems that support public safety without relying on intrusive visual surveillance alone.
  • Extending intelligent sensing to applications such as hazard detection, crowd safety, and resilient security monitoring.
