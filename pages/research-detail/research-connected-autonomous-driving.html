World Model

We study world models for image/video generation and 3D/4D reconstruction and editing toward spatial intelligence.

World Model

Image/Video Generation

  • Designing generative models that synthesize plausible future worlds and enrich the predictive capacity of intelligent agents.
  • Studying controllable image and video generation from structured conditions, as well as generative image restoration, by leveraging generative foundation models such as Stable Diffusion and FLUX for world simulation and future prediction.
  • Supporting scene completion, what-if reasoning, and foresightful planning by generating realistic observations beyond what is directly seen.

3D/4D Reconstruction & Editing

  • Studying 3D and 4D reconstruction from sparse, multi-view, or partially observed inputs for dynamic scene understanding and spatial intelligence.
  • Building editable world representations that support view synthesis, geometry-aware manipulation, and temporal consistency with 3D foundation models such as VGGT.
  • Connecting reconstruction and editing with world modeling to enable controllable spatial understanding, simulation, and interactive content creation.
