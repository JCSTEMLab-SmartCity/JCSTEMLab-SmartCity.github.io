World Model

We study world models for spatial intelligence through three complementary thrusts: multimodal understanding, image/video generation, and unified understanding and generation.

World Model

Multimodal Understanding

  • Studying multimodal intelligence grounded in language, vision, and 3D structure for richer scene understanding and structured world interpretation.
  • Focusing on perception, chain-of-thought reasoning, and geometric reasoning to connect semantics, visual evidence, and spatial representation.
  • Building unified world models that can understand 3D scenes, capture their dynamics, and support downstream spatial intelligence.

Image/Video Generation

  • Studying controllable and consistent image and video generation from structured conditions and interactions.
  • Leveraging generative foundation models such as Stable Diffusion and FLUX for visual synthesis, generative editing, and restoration.
  • Supporting flexible content creation and refinement for simulation, prediction, and interactive world modeling.

Unified Understanding and Generation

  • Building unified world models that tightly couple multimodal understanding with controllable generation in a shared representation space.
  • Connecting perception, reasoning, prediction, and synthesis so that world understanding can guide generation and generated content can refine understanding.
  • Supporting spatial intelligence with closed-loop world modeling for simulation, interaction, and adaptive decision making.
